GPS Para El Cielo

Conectando hogares, alcanzando vidas.

A free app that helps believers pray for every household in their neighborhood by name, visit and invite their neighbors, and follow up so no one is forgotten.

What you can do

🏠

Your neighborhood

Save your home address and see the households nearest to you, on a list and on a map.

🙏

Pray by name

Pray for each family on your street and mark them as prayed for.

🎯

Daily goals and rounds

Set a daily prayer goal and follow your progress until every neighbor has been prayed for, then start a new round.

🚪

Visits and invitations

Record the homes you have visited or invited to church.

📅

Follow-ups and notes

Schedule follow-up dates and keep private notes that only you can see.

🔔

Notifications

Receive reminders and ministry news, kept in an in-app inbox.

Getting started

  1. Create an account with your email, Google or Apple.
  2. Enter your home address, or use your current location.
  3. Choose your daily prayer goal and start praying for the homes around you.

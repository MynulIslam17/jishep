Support

Need help with GPS Para El Cielo? Find answers below or contact us.

Contact us

Email office@gaccchurch.org. Please include the email address you use in the app and, if possible, your device model.

Signed-in users can also write to us from the app: Profile > Contact support.

Common questions

How do I create an account or sign in?

Open the app and choose Sign Up to create an account with your name, email and a password, or continue with Google or Apple. After that, use Sign In with the same method.

I forgot my password.

On the Sign In screen tap "Forgot your password?", enter your email address and follow the link we email you to choose a new password. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive. If you signed up with Google or Apple, sign in with that button instead; there is no separate password.

Google or Apple sign-in does not work.

Make sure you are connected to the internet and signed in to your Google account or Apple ID on the device, then try again. If you chose "Hide My Email" with Apple, your account uses the relay address Apple created, so keep using Sign in with Apple.

How does the app use my location?

Only while you use the app, and only when you choose "Use my current location" to set your home location. The app never tracks you in the background. You can type your address instead.

I denied location access. How do I turn it back on?

Open your device Settings, find GPS Cielo and allow location access "While Using the App". Or simply type your address on the Home screen.

No households appear near me.

Check that your home address is correct (Profile > My address). Then increase the search range in Profile > Prayer preferences. Households are only shown where listings exist, so some areas may have none yet.

How do I change my neighborhood or how many homes I pray for?

Go to Profile > Prayer preferences to set your daily prayer goal, the number of homes in your neighborhood and the search range. You can also search another place on the Home screen without changing your home address.

I do not receive notifications.

Allow notifications for GPS Cielo in your device Settings and make sure you are signed in. Notifications you receive also appear in the inbox (the bell on the Home screen).

Who can see my notes and activity?

Only you. Your notes, prayer and visit records and follow-ups are private to your account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Profile > Delete account. See Delete your account for the steps and what is deleted.

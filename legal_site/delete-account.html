Delete your account

You can permanently delete your GPS Para El Cielo account and its data at any time, directly in the app.

Steps

  1. Open GPS Para El Cielo and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm who you are: enter your password if you use email sign-in, or confirm with Google or Apple if you signed in with them.
  5. Tap Delete. The app returns to the Sign In screen and shows "Account Deleted".

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, Google or Apple login).
  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo, church affiliation, home address and location, and prayer preferences.
  • Your prayer, visit, invitation and follow-up records and your notes.
  • Your in-app notifications and the push notification tokens for your devices.
  • Messages you sent through Contact support.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Data is removed from our database right away; our service provider, Google, may take some time to remove it completely from its systems.

What is not deleted

  • Household listings shown in the app. They are not part of your account and other users keep seeing them.
  • Data held by Google or Apple for your own Google account or Apple ID, which you manage with them.

Can't open the app?

Email office@gaccchurch.org from the address you use in the app and ask us to delete your account. We may need to verify that the account is yours before deleting it.

See also the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Global Alliance of Christian Churches, Inc. ("Global Alliance of Christian Churches, Inc.", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the GPS Para El Cielo mobile app (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

GPS Para El Cielo is a faith-based outreach app provided by Global Alliance of Christian Churches, Inc., a fellowship of churches in the United States (https://gaccchurch.org). We are responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at support@gpscielochurch.org or 1200 Main Street, Suite 400, Houston, TX 77002, United States.

2. Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • Profile details: profile photo, church affiliation, home address and the home location you save, and your prayer preferences (daily goal, cycle goal, number of homes and search range).
  • Outreach activity: the households you mark as prayed for, visited or invited, follow-up dates, and any notes or resident names you choose to enter.
  • Support messages: what you write to us through Contact Support, together with your account email.

3. Sign in with Google or Apple

If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider shares your name, email address and an account identifier with us. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email; we then receive a private relay address from Apple. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

4. Location

With your permission, the App reads your device's precise location only while you are using it, when you choose to use your current location, to set your home location and show households near you. The App does not track your location in the background and we do not keep a history of your movements. We store only the home location and address you save. You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings and enter an address instead.

5. Notifications and device information

If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token for your device and its platform (iOS or Android) so we can send prayer reminders, follow-up reminders and ministry news. Your notifications are also kept in your in-app inbox. Signing out or deleting your account removes the token. Our service providers may also process technical data such as your IP address, device type and operating system version in order to run the service.

6. Information about households and neighbors

The App shows listings of nearby households so that members can pray for their neighbors. A listing contains a street address, an approximate map location and, in some cases, a resident or family name. Listings come from information we compile from publicly available sources and from participating churches. Your own notes, statuses and follow-ups about a household are private to your account; other users cannot see them.

Please enter only what is necessary for prayer and outreach, and never record sensitive details such as health, finances or immigration status. If you live at a listed address and would like it removed or corrected, email us at support@gpscielochurch.org and we will respond promptly.

7. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To show households near your home location and track your prayer and outreach progress.
  • To send the notifications you allow.
  • To answer your support messages.
  • To keep the App safe, prevent abuse, fix problems and improve it.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

8. Service providers we share with

We share information only with providers that help us run the App, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, database storage and push notifications.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: if you choose them to sign in.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: map images. Loading the map sends your IP address and the map area being viewed to its tile servers.
  • Photon by komoot: address search. The text you type in an address field, or the coordinates being looked up, is sent to find matching addresses.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public or Global Alliance of Christian Churches, Inc., or as part of a merger or transfer of the ministry, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

9. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Profile > Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, outreach history and notes, notifications, device tokens and support messages. Copies may remain in secure backups for a limited time before being overwritten.

10. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, with access rules that let only you read and change your own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong password and keep it private.

11. Your choices and rights

  • View and update your profile, address and preferences in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account and data in the App.
  • Turn location access or notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@gpscielochurch.org.

Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states with privacy laws, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights, such as to know what we collect, to access, correct, delete or export it, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to complain to a data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

12. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us at support@gpscielochurch.org and we will delete it.

13. Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App before they take effect.

15. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: support@gpscielochurch.org, or Global Alliance of Christian Churches, Inc., 1200 Main Street, Suite 400, Houston, TX 77002, United States.
